One 1x1 image every 200 px down the page: the first loads with the page and is the view, the rest are lazy, so the browser asks for each as the reader nears it, and one more sits at the foot. Signal's server counts them with no script, no cookie and no id. It is the first thing inside , and body is `position: relative` (bs-sticky-footer.css), so the ladder spans the page's height and a rung past the end of a short page is never asked for.
